# Eight places for proven peers

BCHN provides eight manual connection slots in addition to its usual ten automatic outbound connections. Bitcoin Cash Lab uses those eight slots to keep peers that have earned their place through measured block delivery.

**Your strongest peers are remembered across restarts.** When BCHN comes back online, Bitcoin Cash Lab restores your saved manual selection so BCHN can reconnect to those peers straight away. You keep the set you have built instead of starting the search from scratch.

The automatic outbound connections keep the search moving: rotation disconnects candidates that never deliver first, and BCHN looks for replacements. See [Why 50 blocks?](why-50-blocks.md) for the rule.

This gives the two groups different jobs: the manual set holds on to proven peers, while the automatic connections keep introducing new candidates.
